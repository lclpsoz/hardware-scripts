Notifications

Text, priority and repeat of each notification. You can use {min} (minutes sitting), {snooze}, {steps} and {stale}. Priority 1–2 is silent, 3 plays the app's default sound, 4 adds vibration, 5 is the loudest. Each level can have its own sound in Android's settings for the ntfy app. Test sends the saved version, so press Save first.

A level's phone alarm is a 1-second timer in the phone's Clock app (or a spoken phrase) that rings on the alarm volume. Give a lower volume and a longer interval to the earlier levels so the last level is clearly the most intense. The timer needs "Display over other apps" for Termux in Android settings; labels can have emojis and {min} (minutes sitting) and {time}. Test on a level also triggers its alarm, at the phone's next report.

Long periods (for example you left the phone at home): a Clock timer keeps ringing until it stops by itself, so a new one is never started sooner than the ring time below. Level 4 repeats back off (each wait doubles, up to the maximum), and after the loud phase all sound stops and only the quiet "Still sitting" notification repeats, until you move.

The timer volume is up to the Clock app: the Pi sets the phone's alarm volume, but some Clock apps (Samsung's, for example) play timers at a volume of their own, set inside the Clock app. Google's Clock (com.google.android.deskclock) follows the phone's alarm volume. Android has no global choice of timer app, so name it here.

Settings apply at once and survive restarts.
